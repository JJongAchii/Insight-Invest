# ADR 0030 — Judge proposed evidence, not a self-authored explanation

Date: 2026-09-14. Status: implemented; real-source qualification pending.

## Defect and decision

The v3 boundary reader constructed an object/work/result card and then judged its
own abstraction. Mini and the fixed GPT-5.4 diagnostic both accepted the same EM
ETF product profile. Structural completeness did not establish disclosed analytic
work. Those failures and the frozen original labels remain unchanged.

Replace that repeated classification, not add another paid stage. The v7 selector
still proposes literal transferable-insight and method passages. The second call
receives the original and those passage IDs, but no first-reader category, reason,
verdict, generated summary or explanation card. It checks the role of EACH proposed
passage. It cannot repair an insufficient proposal with another source sentence.
The full body is used separately to establish the central subject.

Code admits an investment subject only when at least one proposal discloses an
analytic operation, comparison, or explanatory mechanism. These are alternatives,
not an all-criteria gate. Objectives, portfolio profiles, asserted benefits and
business/policy explanations do not qualify. No publisher/ETF/ESG blacklist and no
equation, complete strategy or backtest requirement. A model cannot supply an
overall admission verdict. Every raw core, including empirical labels, is checked.

This remains a fallible semantic model check, not a deterministic proof of meaning.
An insufficient proposal can hold a good original: that is an extraction/selection
failure, not evidence that the original is bad. Do not hide that false negative or
turn the held source into a rejected/unreadable document. Existing source-bound
manual audits remain separately identified and never count as automatic success.

## Compatibility and safety

- Boundary v4 fingerprints bind the exact literal proposal as well as source,
  parser, scope, model and settings. Old v1/v2/v3 receipts remain stored, not reused.
- Keep GPT-5 mini, medium, 4,096 output tokens and existing rates; no model migration.
- Same one-request boundary stage, pre-call reservation, actual usage settlement,
  durable failed reservations, cache recovery, retry limit and single-writer budget.
- No first-stage regeneration when its current cache exists. No writer/reviewer
  changes. No direct feed rewrite, user-state deletion, notification or schedule edit.
- Publisher reconstructs proposal-bound receipts against the refetched original;
  diagnostic model reports still cannot seed production.

## Frozen validation before new paid calls

Use ALL existing 13 original reading cases and the two previously frozen extension
cases in scripts/fixtures/research-reading-samples.json. Expected outcomes remain
8 core / 7 non-core across 15 distinct originals. These are development regressions,
not a fresh holdout. Report unique documents, raw selection failures and final
automatic outcomes separately; duplicate sample groups are not extra evidence.

Sequence: products, boundaries, commercialization, subject, subject-positive,
methods, extension. Reuse paid caches for repeated originals. Inspect actual
proposed quotes and roles, not just the summary status. The ETF and SaaS failures
must be blocked while useful AQR/CFM/D.E.Shaw/Robeco/RAFI/Verdad research remains.
Complete the fixed panel even if a semantic mismatch occurs, provided no transport,
source-binding or budget failure requires stopping. Do not retune during this panel.

At most 21 new selection/boundary calls in this panel, no summary/model comparison
calls. Existing aggregate monthly cap is unchanged at $2 ($1 production, $1
qualification). Before snapshot: qualification reserved/spent $0.72436770;
remaining $0.27563230. No ledger reset, retroactive refund or automatic cap increase.
All calls execute committed code in the isolated GitHub Actions qualification job.
No production release solely from synthetic unit tests or a green API contract.

Additional unseen-source validation, if needed for release, must freeze originals
and expectations before calling this version and remain within the same budget.
